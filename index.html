The list uses your clean passes to estimate comfortable reading and physical demands, then looks for maps that offer a manageable challenge without ranking them by pp. Difficulty is calculated with the shown mods, and repeated failed attempts exclude a map.

A trial map has less direct evidence for its mod setup, so it is a cautious suggestion rather than established ability. For normal-speed trials, Difficulty Adjust changes only AR to your inferred reading range while keeping the map’s original speed and other settings.

Use the accuracy goal as a starting point and move on after a couple of attempts. If you cannot follow the patterns, mark the map as Too hard so future recommendations become easier; detailed technique diagnosis would require pattern and replay analysis.
